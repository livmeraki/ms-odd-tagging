Scenario: waiting_for_pedestrian_to_cross
Definition: Ego is stopped or moving slowly because a pedestrian is crossing, entering, or clearly intending to enter ego's expected path.
Do not tag based only on pedestrian proximity or ego stopping.
Require a plausible pedestrian-ego path conflict and temporal ego response.
Evaluate only this scenario for the supplied window.

Motion-state semantics:
- The motion-state label "stationary" MUST be interpreted as "stopped".
- "stationary" satisfies any condition requiring ego to be stopped, stopping, stopped or moving slowly.
- Never reject a candidate only because the motion-state label is "stationary" instead of the word "stopped".
- "stationary" alone is NOT sufficient for a positive decision.

waiting_for_pedestrian_to_cross=true still requires all of:
1. a pedestrian crossing or plausibly about to cross the ego path,
2. a plausible pedestrian-ego path conflict,
3. ego response consistent with yielding/waiting: slowing, stopping, or stationary,
4. temporal/geometric evidence that the ego response is related to that pedestrian.

Valid negative reasoning may say:
"Ego is stationary, which satisfies the stopped condition, but there is insufficient evidence of pedestrian-ego path conflict or that the stop is caused by the pedestrian."

Return JSON with:
recording_id, window_start_frame, window_end_frame, scenario, decision, confidence,
event_start_frame, event_end_frame, primary_object_ids, evidence_ids, reason,
ambiguities, insufficient_evidence, review_required.

For a positive decision, at least one pedestrian object ID must be in primary_object_ids.
