Scenario: starting_u_turn
Definition: Ego is beginning a U-turn maneuver, with ego heading changing toward the opposite travel direction.
Evaluate only this scenario for the supplied window.

Use the structured ego trajectory, heading-change evidence, and BEV images as verification evidence.
The deterministic candidate generator is only a high-recall prefilter; it may include sharp turns, curves, lane changes, or noisy heading changes.

starting_u_turn=true requires:
1. ego motion consistent with the beginning of a U-turn, not merely a normal curve or lane change,
2. substantial ego heading change toward reversal of direction,
3. spatial trajectory/BEV evidence that ego is turning across or around the roadway rather than following a normal bend,
4. event frames restricted to the portion of the window where the U-turn starts.

Return false or insufficient evidence when:
- the ego follows an ordinary curve,
- the heading change is small or noisy,
- the motion is a simple left/right turn rather than a U-turn,
- the BEV/trajectory does not support a reversal maneuver.

Return JSON with:
recording_id, window_start_frame, window_end_frame, scenario, decision, confidence,
event_start_frame, event_end_frame, primary_object_ids, evidence_ids, reason,
ambiguities, insufficient_evidence, review_required.
