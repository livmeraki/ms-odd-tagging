Scenario: on_traffic_light_intersection
Definition: Ego is spatially inside an effective intersection footprint that is controlled by traffic lights or signal evidence.
Evaluate only this scenario for the supplied window.

Use the structured intersection topology, traffic-light evidence, lane/boundary changes, ego motion, and BEV images as verification evidence.
The deterministic candidate generator is only a high-recall prefilter; traffic-light evidence or topology colors alone are not final proof.

on_traffic_light_intersection=true requires:
1. ego is spatially inside the effective intersection footprint,
2. the intersection has supplied traffic-light or signal evidence,
3. connected-road/lane-opening evidence supports an intersection rather than an approach road, ordinary curve, or simple merge/split,
4. the positive decision is based only on supplied evidence IDs and BEV images.

Do not require unrelated dynamic objects.
Return false or insufficient evidence when ego is only approaching, has already exited, or when traffic-light evidence is present but the ego is not inside an intersection footprint.

Return JSON with:
recording_id, window_start_frame, window_end_frame, scenario, decision, confidence,
event_start_frame, event_end_frame, primary_object_ids, evidence_ids, reason,
ambiguities, insufficient_evidence, review_required.
